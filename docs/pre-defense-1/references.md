# Bibliography — BuildAI 3D Pre-Defense 1

How these were checked : every scholarly record's title, authors, year, venue, pages/article number and DOI was confirmed against the **Crossref REST API** (`https://api.crossref.org/works/<DOI>`). Abstract-level claims come from the publisher, arXiv or ACL Anthology pages, or from a full reading where noted in [03_literature_review.md](03_literature_review.md). Product and technical pages were opened on 2026-10-20. Nothing here is unverified.

Citation keys (`[S1]`, `[T1]`, `[P1]`) are used across the package.
